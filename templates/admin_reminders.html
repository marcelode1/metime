First-time Twilio setup — how to
  1. Create an account at twilio.com and verify your email & phone.
  2. In the Twilio Console, buy a phone number with SMS capability (about $1–2/month).
  3. Complete A2P 10DLC registration (required for US texting) — Console will prompt you; approval usually takes a day or two. Without it, US carriers block business texts.
  4. From the Console dashboard, copy your Account SID and Auth Token into the fields above, and enter your Twilio number as the From number in +1 format.
  5. Click Save Settings, send yourself a test text, then turn on Send automatic text reminders.

Note: clients who marked "no reminders" on their intake are skipped, and every message includes "Reply STOP to opt out." Texting costs about a penny per message on Twilio.
